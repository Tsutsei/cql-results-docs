_Last updated: 10 November 2026_

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the [Terms of use]({{ '/terms/' | relative_url }}) between the customer installing **CQL Results – Search Tables for Confluence** ("Customer", controller) and **Bc. Tomáš Buchta, sole trader, IČO 21540951, 30. dubna 2307/12, 702 00 Ostrava, Czech Republic** ("Vendor", processor), and applies to personal data processed by the App under Article 28 of the GDPR.

1. **Subject matter and duration.** Searching the Customer's Confluence site and displaying the results on a search page, in page macros and in page exports, for as long as the App is installed.
2. **Nature and purpose.** Running Confluence searches (CQL) with the viewing user's permissions and returning the results (titles, spaces, names of owners and editors, dates, labels, excerpts) to that user's browser or page export. Nothing is stored.
3. **Types of personal data.** Display names of content owners, creators and editors, and personal data contained in titles and excerpts of the results. The data is processed in transit only and is not stored by the App.
4. **Data subjects.** Users of the Customer's Confluence site and people named in its content.
5. **Instructions.** The Vendor processes personal data only to operate the App as used by the Customer and on documented instructions sent to [support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net](mailto:support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net).
6. **Where data is processed.** Exclusively within Atlassian's Forge platform (Key-Value Store) for the Customer's site, inheriting Atlassian's data residency. The App sends no data outside Atlassian. The Vendor has no access to the stored data.
7. **Sub-processor.** Atlassian (provider of the Forge platform), under Atlassian's own terms and data processing addendum. The Vendor will announce any new sub-processor on this page in advance.
8. **Security.** Data is protected by the security measures of the Atlassian Forge platform (encryption at rest and in transit, tenant isolation). Content is copied with the user's own permissions and never stored by the App.
9. **Confidentiality.** Only the Vendor works on the App and is bound by confidentiality.
10. **Assistance.** The Vendor assists the Customer with data subject requests and data protection obligations as far as the App's design allows (the App stores no data; content is managed in Confluence).
11. **Personal data breaches.** The Vendor notifies the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a breach affecting the App.
12. **Deletion.** The App stores no Customer data, so there is nothing to delete or return on uninstallation.
13. **Audits.** The Vendor makes available the information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA on request.
14. **Governing law.** As in the Terms of use.
