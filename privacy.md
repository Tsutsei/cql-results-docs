_Last updated: 10 November 2026_

This policy describes how the Atlassian Marketplace app "CQL Results – Search Tables for Confluence" (the "App") processes data. The App is provided by **Tsutsei Software**, a brand of the sole trader identified on the [Legal information](https://tsutsei-software.com/legal.html) page ("we"). Contact: **[support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net](mailto:support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net)**.

## 1. What the App stores
**Nothing.** The App has no database and no storage. The settings of a CQL Results macro (the query, the columns) are part of the Confluence page, like the settings of any macro. A search shared as a link is held in the link itself.

## 2. What the App reads
When a user opens the CQL Search page, views a page with a CQL Results macro or exports such a page, the App runs the search in Confluence **with that user's permissions** and returns the results – titles, spaces, names of owners, creators and editors, dates, labels, excerpts, file types and sizes – to that user's browser or export. The App cannot create, change or delete content.

## 3. Where the data is processed
Searches run on Atlassian's infrastructure (Forge). The App does not send data to any server outside Atlassian, and we (the vendor) have no access to it.

## 4. Purpose and legal basis
Data is processed solely to provide the App's functions on behalf of the Confluence site, which acts as the controller; we act as processor (Art. 28 GDPR) where personal data is involved (for example names of content owners).

## 5. Retention
The App retains no data. Technical logs kept by Atlassian Forge contain error messages only, never queries or results.

## 6. Data Processing Addendum
The [Data Processing Addendum]({{ '/dpa/' | relative_url }}) (Art. 28 GDPR) applies to all customers.

## 7. Sharing
We do not sell or share data. Sub-processor: Atlassian (hosting of the Forge platform).

## 8. Your rights
Content stays in Confluence and is managed there. For requests concerning personal data, contact your Confluence site admin or us at [support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net](mailto:support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net).

## 9. Changes
We will publish changes to this policy on this page.
